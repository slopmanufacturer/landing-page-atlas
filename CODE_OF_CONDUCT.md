# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. This project is a catalogue of design work, and the people who
contribute to it are doing it for free. Critique the page, not the person.

## Expected behaviour

- Give feedback that a stranger can act on: name the element, the width, the
  browser, and what you expected.
- Assume the person on the other side is trying to do good work.
- Accept that a maintainer may decline a design. The atlas has a point of
  view; not every good page fits it.
- Respect the house rules in `AGENTS.md`, including the ban on real brands,
  trackers, and frameworks.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in issues, pull requests, or
  discussions.
- Discriminatory language or imagery of any kind.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Submitting work that is not yours to license, or that impersonates a real
  company.
- Sustained disruption of a thread after a maintainer has asked you to stop.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and any space where you
are representing the project.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainer through GitHub:
<https://github.com/vezeqs>. Reports are read and handled discreetly.
Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, or block accounts.
