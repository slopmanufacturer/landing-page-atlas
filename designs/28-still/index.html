01 / Practice

Three ways
to breathe.

Pick the one that fits the day. The circle expands on the inhale and contracts on the exhale. That is the whole instruction.

01

4—7—8

In for four, hold for seven, out for eight. Good for the end of the day.

02

Box

Four in, four hold, four out, four hold. Good for a loud room.

03

Coherent

Five in, five out. No holds. Good for the middle of anything.

02 / About

No streaks.
No badges.

Still is a small timer, not a habit tracker. It does not know how many days you have practised, because it does not ask. There is no account and nothing is stored.

A minute is enough. Two is also enough. Stopping is allowed.
