Cuvée 9 / 2019 / 12% vol.

01 / The wine

Three notes,
in order.

60% Chardonnay, 40% Pinot Noir. Nine months in stainless, then three years on the lees. Dosage: none.

01

Brioche

Warm bread, almond, a little salt. The lees doing their slow work.

02

Citrus

Lemon peel and grapefruit pith. The Chardonnay keeping the room bright.

03

Chalk

Wet stone, white pepper, a finish that stays longer than the glass.
