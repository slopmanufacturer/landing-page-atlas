01 / Counters

Three streams, six counters.

Your ticket letter tells you where to sit. If you picked the wrong service, tell a steward; they will swap the ticket rather than send you to the back of the queue.

A

Council tax & benefits

Counters 1—2
  • Council tax bills, discounts, and single-person reduction
  • Housing benefit and council tax reduction
  • Payment arrangements, before 16:45

Bring Your bill reference, or the last four digits of your National Insurance number.

B

Permits & passes

Counters 3—4
  • Blue badge, new applications and renewals
  • Concessionary bus pass
  • Residents' parking permits and visitor books

Bring Photo ID and proof of address. Renewals: bring the old badge, and come before 15:30.

C

Housing & general

Counters 5—6
  • Housing repairs and tenancy questions
  • Pest control bookings
  • Allotment waiting list and registrar appointments

Bring Your tenancy number, or the address the repair is about. Repairs are logged here and fixed by the repair team, who ring within two working days.

Not at the counter

Payments after 16:45, marriage ceremonies, and school admissions. Admissions are handled at the Guildhall, which has its own queue and its own coffee.

Wrong stream

It happens. A steward will reissue the ticket without sending you to the back. We would rather move one ticket than argue about it.

02 / Visit

In the room.

The building was a co-operative store until 1991, which is why the ceilings are high and the doors are heavy. The queue is on the ground floor; the quiet room is not.

Saturday is permits and passes only. The last ticket is issued 20 minutes before closing, and the machine is firm about it.

The house rules

  • NumbersYour number is called three times, then the board moves on. If you miss it, take a fresh ticket; the steward will try to slot you in.
  • Quiet roomSecond door on the left, no ticket needed. It is for forms, phone calls, and sitting down.
  • Hearing loopCounters 1 and 4. Tell the steward if you would rather sign; two of them can.

Privacy and access

  • Privacy screensEvery counter has one. If you need longer than a screen allows, ask for the side room.
  • Step-freeLevel entrance from Market Square, lift to the first floor, accessible toilet on the ground floor.
  • ChildrenThere is a box of pencils by the door. It has never run out, and nobody knows who refills it.

03 / Book

Skip the queue.

Booked appointments go straight to counters 3 and 6, which handle permits, passes, and the general stream. Council tax and benefits stay walk-in only; those conversations take as long as they take, and we will not rush them to fit a slot.

We confirm by email within one working day. If the day you want is full, we will offer the next one rather than leave you guessing.
