02 Observation log

Selected nights, 2026

  1. 2026.10.19 First light

    The mirror held collimation through a six-hour run.

    We watched the Ring Nebula until the dew won. At 03:12 the secondary mirror fogged, so we made tea and let the dome track nothing in particular.

    Seeing 0.9″
  2. 2026.09.30 Cloud

    A night of bad seeing, kept anyway.

    Clouds at 2,400 metres. We opened the dome for the sound of it: rain on aluminium, the shutter ticking, the kettle cooling slowly on the desk.

    Seeing 3.4″
  3. 2026.08.14 Unaided

    Forty-one meteors between 01:10 and 03:00.

    Perseids. We counted out loud from the roof, wrapped in the observatory's one good blanket. Nobody wrote anything down until morning.

    Naked eye
