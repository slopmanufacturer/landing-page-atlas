01 / Sorting

What goes where.

If it is not on this list, it does not go in a bin. Batteries and paint go to the household recycling centre on Sizergh Road; both have started fires in the lorry, and we would like to stop that.

  • Tea bags

    Squeeze them out. The bag, not the tea.

    Food caddy

  • Coffee grounds

    Straight in, filter paper and all. The paper breaks down.

    Food caddy

  • Greasy pizza boxes

    Torn in half if they do not fit. They always fit.

    Black bin

  • Nappies

    Never in the blue bin. Never in the green bin. This has been tested.

    Black bin

  • Crisp packets

    One day a scheme will take them. Until then, general waste.

    Black bin

  • Broken glass

    Wrapped in newspaper, taped shut. Not loose, and not in the blue bin.

02 / Rounds

Fourteen rounds, three days.

The depot runs Monday to Friday, but most of the borough is collected Tuesday, Thursday, and Friday. Find your street above; the round below tells you who to ring when something goes wrong.

Round A Tuesday

Abbot's Walk, Brindle Road, Gallows Hill, Junction Row, and 41 other streets.

Crew 4 / depot bay 2 / 07:00—15:30

Round B Thursday

Colmoor Lane, Dovecote Street, Hartley Croft, and 58 other streets.

Crew 7 / depot bay 4 / 07:00—15:30

Round C Friday

Eller Beck Rise, Fenn Street, Ivy Bank, and 46 other streets.

Crew 9 / depot bay 1 / 07:00—16:00

Bank holidays

Every collection that week runs one day late, including the Friday round, which runs on Saturday. Christmas week is posted on the depot gate in December.

Assisted collections

If you cannot get the bin to the kerb, we will come to the door. Ring the depot; there is no charge and no assessment visit.

Garden waste

The green bin runs from March to November, on your usual day. The rest of the year it is a very large, very empty bin.

03 / Report

Missed a bin?

Tell us by 15:00 the next working day and we will come back within two working days. If the bin was out by 07:00 and we missed it, there is nothing to pay. If it was not out, we will say so, politely.

Ring 01632 960 214 if the form is not working, or if the bin has been missed twice in a row. That is a round problem, not a form problem.

We reply by email within one working day, usually sooner.
