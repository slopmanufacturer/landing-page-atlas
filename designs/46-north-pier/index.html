02 / Fares

Three ways
to pay.

Cash or card at the pier hut. No booking fee, because there is no booking.

  • Single £3.20 One crossing, one direction. Child under five free.
  • Return £5.60 Valid for fourteen days. Come back when the weather suits.
  • Season £48 A calendar month, any sailing, any direction.

Bicycle £1. Dog free. Luggage over 20 kg £2.

03 / The crossing

A short hop
with a long memory.

The route has run since 1908, when the pier was timber and the boat was rowed. The current vessel, MV Carrow, carries forty-two passengers and a postbag. The crossing takes twelve minutes in still water, eighteen when the wind is against the tide.

  • Step-free Both slips are ramped. The boat takes a wheelchair at the stern.
  • Dogs On a lead, on the deck, not in the cabin.
  • Tides Low water can shorten the slip. Listen for the horn.
