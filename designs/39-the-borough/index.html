03 / Club

A club,
not a brand.

The Borough has played on the same patch of south-east London since 1898. The stand was rebuilt in 1954, the tea hut in 2009, and the pitch every June. Nobody has ever tried to sell the naming rights.

We run two senior sides, a Sunday veterans team, and a youth section that has produced three of the first team. The bar opens after the final whistle and closes when the last person leaves.
