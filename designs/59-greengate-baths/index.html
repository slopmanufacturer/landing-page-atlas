03 / Visit

The house rules.

Most of these are older than the building's restoration and one of them is older than the building. None of them are negotiable at the desk.

Opening hours

Last entry 45 minutes before close. The pool is cleared 20 minutes before close, which is when the whistles start.

The whistles

  • One long blastOut of the water, no questions. It has happened twice this year, both times a fire alarm.
  • Two short blastsThe session ends in five minutes. Finish the length you are on, not the next one.
  • Three short blastsClear a lane for a lifeguard. You will see why.

Lane etiquette

  • ClockwiseLanes run clockwise. The fast lane is the one with the triathletes; the slow lane is the one with the rest of us.
  • OvertakingTap the foot in front, pass on the right. Do not tap a stranger's foot to make a point.
  • Lane ropesDo not hang on them. They are older than the boiler and twice as tired.

Access and the slide

  • Poolside hoistMain pool, by the shallow end. Ask any lifeguard; no booking.
  • Changing room 3Accessible, with a shower and a changing bench. Radar key, or ask at the desk.
  • The slideClosed since 2019, awaiting a part that is no longer manufactured. The sign stays up out of respect.

04 / Learn to swim

Start them early.

Lessons run in blocks of ten, in the small pool, with two teachers and a lifeguard who is not allowed to look away. Children join from four. Adults join from whenever they stopped being embarrassed, which is usually around 35.

The waiting list is 48 children long. Places come up at half term and we write to you the same day, so check the email you actually read.

We reply when a place opens, usually within six weeks.
