02 / About

One transmitter.
No algorithm.

Signal has been broadcasting from a converted coastguard hut since 2017. We run 400 watts, a borrowed aerial, and a schedule made by people who live here.

You can hear us from the harbour to about ten miles inland. After that, you are on your own. That is part of the charm.
