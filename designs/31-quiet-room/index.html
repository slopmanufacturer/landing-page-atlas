01 / Exhibition

Three works.
One at a time.

Until 20 December 2026
Wednesday—Sunday, 12:00—18:00

The room is 4 by 6 metres. The work changes every eight weeks. There is no wall text, only a folded sheet by the door with the title, the date, and one paragraph.

The current cycle brings together three works made between 2024 and 2026. They are not a series. They are three things that seemed to belong in the same quiet room.

Free entry. No booking. No photography after 17:00.
