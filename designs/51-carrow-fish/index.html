02 / Auction

Five o'clock,
then six.

Buyers walk the slab from five. The auctioneer starts at six and works the room in lots of one box or one tub. Cash settles the same morning; account buyers settle on Friday.

  1. 05:00

    Doors open. Buyers inspect the boxes, check the ice, and mark what they want.

  2. 06:00

    Auction starts. Lots go in the order they were landed, not by species.

  3. 06:40

    First vans leave. Local restaurants collect from the side door until eight.

  4. 08:00

    Slab cleared. Anything unsold goes to the ice house or the chippy on the corner.

All under 12 metres, all day boats, all back in before the tide turns.

  • Kestrel Skipper Ada Finch / 10.6 m trawler Sole and plaice. Lands Tuesday and Friday, weather permitting.
