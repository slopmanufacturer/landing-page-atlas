01 / Menu

Five courses,
no choices.

The kitchen cooks one menu for the table. Tell us about allergies when you book; the vegetarian menu is cooked alongside.

November menu. Subject to what the market has, which is the only honest way to write it.

Wine pairing, five glasses, £32. Or bring a bottle for £12 corkage.

  1. I

    Oyster, apple, horseradish

    Raw, cold, and gone in two bites.

  2. II

    Celeriac soup, brown butter

    Thick enough to stand a spoon in. Bread from the bakery on Quay Street.

  3. III

    Hake, mussels, cider

    Landed that morning, cooked in a wide pan, served with the liquor.

  4. IV

    Venison, beetroot, juniper

    Roe deer from the estate above the sound. Pink, rested, sliced thick.

  5. V

    Damson tart, cream

    Damsons from the hedge behind the pub. The last of the season.

03 / Book

How it works,
and what it costs.

Email with the date and how many of you. We reply within two days with a payment link; the seat is held for forty-eight hours. £68 a head, wine extra, and a £20 deposit if you cancel inside a week.
