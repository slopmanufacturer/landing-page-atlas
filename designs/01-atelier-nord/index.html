Studio

We are six architects, two model-makers, and one very old plotter. We work slowly, draw by hand first, and measure twice.

Our buildings begin with a question about weather: where does the snow settle, where does the low sun land in February, which window will be too bright in June? The answers are rarely digital. They come from standing on the site until the hands are cold.

We keep a small practice on purpose. Every project is drawn by the people who will visit it during construction, and every material is chosen for how it ages rather than how it photographs. A building should be better in its twentieth year than its first.
