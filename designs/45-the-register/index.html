02 / Visit

In the reading room.

Bring a pencil. We will lend you one if you forget, but the rule is older than either of us.

Address

41 Cinder Lane
Leeds LS2 7BQ

Five minutes from the bus station, on the canal side.

Hours

Tue—Fri 10:00—16:30
Sat 10:00—13:00
Sun—Mon closed

Late opening by appointment, Thursdays until 19:00.

Reader's ticket

Free. Bring photo ID and proof of address. We issue it while you wait, usually under ten minutes.

Under 16s need an adult with them in the reading room.

Lockers

£1 coin, returned. Bags larger than a sheet of A3 stay at the desk.

Pencils only

No ink in the reading room. Laptops and cameras without flash are fine.

Ordering

Items from the strongroom need 24 hours. Order by 16:00 for same-day reading.

Step-free

Entrance on Cinder Lane, lift to the second floor. Assistance dogs welcome.

Questions before you visit: records@the-register.example · 0113 000 0189

03 / Request

Ask for a record.

Give us the reference code if you have it. If you do not, describe what you are looking for and we will search the index for you. We answer within three working days.

Photocopies 15p per sheet. Scans £4 per item. We do not copy anything fragile, and we do not guess at dates.

Record request / Form 4 Office use only

We reply within three working days, usually sooner.
