The Site

A 1930s lido, a bandstand, and a car park we have permission to use. We took all three. The pool is heated. The sea is not.

Saltdean Lido
Saltdean Park Road
Brighton BN2 7FR

  1. 01 Swim between sets. Towels are not a personality.
  2. 02 No lanyards, no VIP pens, no corporate sponsors.
  3. 03 Sunscreen is free at the canteen. Use it.

Getting here. 20 minutes from Brighton station on the 12A. Last bus back is 00:40 — or walk the cliff path and miss it.
