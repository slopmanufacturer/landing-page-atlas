02 / The week

What a
course feels like.

Based on the Level 2 week. Times move with the tide; the order does not.

  1. Mon

    Rigging, safety, and the first reach across the sound. Ashore by four.

  2. Tue

    Tacking in the channel until it stops being a decision. Theory hour in the shed when the rain comes.

  3. Wed

    Man overboard drills, then lunch at anchor. The afternoon is yours if the wind holds.

  4. Thu

    First passage: North Pier to Inch Island and back. Reefing practice on the return.

  5. Fri

    Assessment, a short solo leg, and the logbook signed at the slip.
