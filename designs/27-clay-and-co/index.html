01 / Process

Three firings.
One object.

Everything is thrown, dried, trimmed, and fired in the same room. The kiln runs on Wednesdays. The studio smells of clay and coffee.

  1. 01

    Throw

    Clay on the wheel, water on the hands. The shape is decided in the first ten minutes.

  2. 02

    Trim

    The next day, the foot is cut and the handle is pulled. This is where it becomes useful.

  3. 03

    Fire

    Bisque, glaze, stoneware. Three days of cooling before anything is allowed to be sold.
