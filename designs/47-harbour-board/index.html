01 / Notices to mariners

Four things
that changed.

Notices stay up for ninety days. Anything older lives in the archive at the office.

  1. 12 / 26

    Inner basin dredging, 3–14 November

    The inner basin is closed to craft over 8 m while the suction dredger works the north wall. Visitor berths 4–9 are suspended. Use the outer basin, or call the office on channel 12.

    Works
  2. 11 / 26

    No. 3 buoy re-established

    The starboard-hand buoy at the entrance has been relaid in position 56° 12.4′ N, 005° 48.1′ W. The temporary green buoy is withdrawn. Charts are correct as of this notice.

    Navigation
  3. 10 / 26

    Winter dues, from 1 November

    Annual mooring dues are £412 for craft up to 9 m, £38 for each metre after. Liveaboards add £96 for water and waste. Invoices go out on the first working day of the month.

    Dues
  4. 09 / 26

    Quay wall ladder out of service

    The ladder at the east end of the quay is being replaced. Land at the slip or the west ladder until the works are signed off. Estimated completion 8 November.

    Works

03 / Office

Call before
you come alongside.

The harbour is not a marina. There is no pontoon, no shower block, and no one on the quay after dark. What there is: deep water, a chandlery that opens at seven, and a harbourmaster who answers the radio.
