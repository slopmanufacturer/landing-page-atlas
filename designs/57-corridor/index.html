02 / The house

What the dues
actually pay for.

The building was a temperance hotel until 1962. We took the lease in 2017, kept the corridor, and put the bar where the reception was. There is no gym, no pool, and no business centre.

  • 01

    The library

    2,000 books, newspapers, and a rule about silence after six.

    18 seats
  • 02

    The bar

    Six stools, one bartender, and a short list that changes on Mondays.

    24 standing
  • 03

    Room 4

    Talks, screenings, and the occasional argument. Projector and screen.

    40 seats
  • 04

    The courtyard

    Open April to October, heated after that, and never booked for parties.

    30 seats
  • 05

    The piano room

    A 1912 upright that is tuned twice a year whether it needs it or not.

    8 seats
  • 06

    The corridor

    Not a room. Somewhere to stand and finish a conversation.
