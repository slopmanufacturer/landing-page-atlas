01 / Route

Eight stops, one line.

Zone 1 is the city centre. Zone 2 starts at Northgate. Zone 3 is the park-and-ride and everything after it. The whole line takes 22 minutes end to end, which is nine minutes faster than the 44 bus.

03 / Fares

Three zones. No surprises.

Buy at the platform machine, tap a contactless card at the gate, or use the app. The fare is the same either way. Cash is not accepted on board.

Zone 1 Eastgate — Victoria Bridge

Four stops in the city centre.

Zone 2 Northgate — Saltworks

Two stops, including the hospital.

04 / Access

Getting on and off.

The line was built in 1998, which means some platforms are easier than others. We would rather tell you which ones than pretend otherwise.

Step-free from street to platform

  • EastgateLift to the bus station and the market.
  • St Anne'sRamp, 1:12, from St Anne's Street.
  • Corn ExchangeLevel entrance from the square.
  • SaltworksLift to both platforms.
  • Bramble HillRamp to platform 1 only; platform 2 is 40 m along the footpath.
  • CalderbankLevel entrance from the car park.

On board

  • SpaceTwo wheelchair spaces per tram, with a 75 mm gap at the door.
  • AnnouncementsAudio next-stop announcements and visual displays on every tram.
  • Dogs and bikesAssistance dogs travel free. Folded bikes are welcome outside 07:30—09:30 and 16:30—18:30.
  • If a lift failsStaff will arrange a taxi to the next step-free stop. Ask at the gate; you will not be charged.

Travel helpline 0300 000 0606, 07:00—20:00 daily. Text relay 18001.
